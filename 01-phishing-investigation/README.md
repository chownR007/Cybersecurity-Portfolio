# Phishing Email Investigation

## Objective

Investigate a simulated phishing email and identify potential indicators of compromise (IOCs).

## Scenario

An employee reports receiving an email claiming to be from Microsoft. The message states that the employee's account will be disabled unless they verify their account immediately.

## Investigation Goals

- Identify suspicious characteristics of the email
- Analyze the sender and domain
- Examine suspicious URLs
- Identify potential indicators of compromise
- Document findings
- Recommend appropriate security actions

## Skills Demonstrated

- Phishing analysis
- Email investigation
- IOC identification
- Domain analysis
- Threat intelligence
- Incident documentation
