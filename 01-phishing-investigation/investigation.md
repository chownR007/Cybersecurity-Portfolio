## 2. Executive Summary

A simulated employee phishing report was received involving an email claiming to be from Microsoft.

The email attempted to create a sense of urgency by informing the recipient that their account would be disabled unless they completed an immediate verification process.

The purpose of this investigation is to determine whether the message contains characteristics commonly associated with phishing and to identify potential indicators of compromise.

---

## 3. Investigation Objectives

The investigation will attempt to:

1. Identify suspicious characteristics of the email.
2. Analyze the sender information.
3. Examine the sending domain.
4. Analyze URLs contained in the message.
5. Identify potential indicators of compromise.
6. Determine the appropriate security response.
7. Document the investigation and findings.

---

## 4. Initial Observations

The following characteristics were identified as potentially suspicious:

- Unexpected account-security notification.
- Urgent language requesting immediate action.
- Threat of account deactivation.
- Request to verify account information.
- Potential use of a suspicious link.
- Possible impersonation of a trusted organization.

These characteristics require additional investigation before determining the final disposition of the message.

## 8. Recommended Response

If this were a real-world security incident, recommended actions could include:

- Do not click links contained in the suspicious email.
- Do not provide credentials or sensitive information.
- Report the message to the organization's security team.
- Preserve the original email and headers for investigation.
- Block confirmed malicious domains or URLs.
- Reset credentials if compromise is suspected.
- Review authentication logs for suspicious activity.
